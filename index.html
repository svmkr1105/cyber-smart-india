About Cyber Smart India

📌 About Website

Cyber Smart India is a digital awareness initiative to educate people about cyber frauds, online scams, and internet safety.

Purpose: To reduce online frauds and financial scams by spreading cyber hygiene practices.

Why important? Cyber awareness is the first line of defense in today's digital world.

Cyber Awareness Topics

Know the threats, learn the defense.

OTP Fraud Awareness

What: Scammers trick you into sharing OTP to drain bank accounts.

Prevention: Never share OTP.

Phishing Awareness

What: Fraudulent emails mimicking legitimate companies.

Prevention: Check sender, enable 2FA.

Social Media Scams

What: Fake giveaways, romance scams, profile cloning.

Prevention: Privacy settings.

Identity Theft

What: Stealing personal data (Aadhaar, PAN).

Prevention: Limit sharing IDs.

Cyberbullying

What: Harassment, trolling, doxxing online.

Prevention: Block, report.

Online Banking Fraud

What: Unauthorized transactions, SIM swap.

Prevention: Use official app.
